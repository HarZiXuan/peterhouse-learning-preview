# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users
Teachers at Peterhouse Preschool (Malaysia/SE Asia) who complete a June and December learning review for each child, and the parents who receive it. Teachers use a local web app on the school computer; parents receive a printed A4 / PDF report and keep it as a keepsake.

## Product Purpose
Turn a teacher's quick ratings of a child (short statements across five areas: English, Chinese, Mathematics, Social-Emotional Development and Self-Enrichment, plus what the child loved most) into a warm, specific end-of-semester Learning Review for families. Success: parents feel their child was seen, and the report is something they want to keep and share.

## Positioning
A school-branded, privacy-first report: the writing service only receives anonymous answers, and photos and names stay on the school computer.

## Operating Context
- Printed A4 (and PDF), ~6 pages, generated by printing `/report/:id` in headless Chrome.
- Five areas, 3–6 statements each (admin-editable, versioned), rated on a fixed scale: Still learning, Sometimes, Regularly, Confidently, Really shines, or Not seen yet. The report never shows numbers, rankings or negative words.
- Staff screens use the Chalk & Crest look (Claude Design canvas "Learning Review Interface"); the printed report keeps the Peterhouse print palette and type below.
- Photos are only used where parents have given photo consent.

## Capabilities and Constraints
- Next.js app, SQLite, data folder holds `learning-review.db` and `photos/`.
- Must stay A4 print-safe with Chrome print colour adjustment.
- Report text comes from the sentence bank or an AI provider; layout must cope with variable paragraph lengths.

## Brand Commitments
Peterhouse crest and colours: red (#ae1d25), gold (#fdcb40), sky (#c7e9fa), blush (#f5ccce), navy (#091f34); Poppins / Jost / Nunito Sans fonts. Confirmed: keep brand colours and crest, but a freer, more playful, photo-led layout.

## Evidence on Hand
Crest and logo in `public/brand/`. Photos: none yet; teachers will upload several photos per child per term (to be built). Do not fabricate photos; designs must degrade gracefully with zero photos.

## Product Principles
1. The child is the hero: photos and their name lead.
2. Warm and specific, never comparative.
3. Works printed first; screen is a preview.
4. Privacy by default: photos only with consent.

## Accessibility & Inclusion
Body text at least 10.5pt in print; sufficient contrast for printed colour; no information conveyed by colour alone.
